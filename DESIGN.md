# NYC Taxi Cyber UI Design System

## Theme & Aesthetic
- **Style**: Cyber / Futuristic / Tech Noir
- **Vibe**: Sci-fi movie HUD (Heads-Up Display), hacking simulator, dark cyberpunk dashboard.
- **Backgrounds**: Deep black (`#050505`) with subtle geometric grids or glowing gradients.
- **Borders**: Sharp, angular edges. Avoid heavy rounding; prefer diagonal cuts (clip-path) or sharp corners.
- **Highlights**: High-contrast neon glow effects (cyan, magenta/pink, electric purple).

## Colors
- **Primary**: Neon Cyan (`#00F3FF`)
- **Secondary**: Cyber Pink / Magenta (`#FF00FF`)
- **Tertiary**: Electric Purple (`#8A2BE2`)
- **Background (Base)**: Deep Space Black (`#050505`)
- **Surface / Card Background**: Dark translucent gray (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.8)`) with cyan or magenta borders.
- **Text (Primary)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or slightly tinted cyan-white (`#E0FFFF`).
- **Text (Muted)**: Steel Gray (`#808080`).

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Orbitron` (for headings, metrics, and aggressive HUD numbers).
- **Secondary Font**: `Space Mono` (for body text, logs, and technical data tables).

## UI Elements (HUD Style)
- **Cards/Containers**: Thin cyan or magenta borders. Use inset box shadows to create glowing inner edges.
- **Buttons**: Transparent backgrounds with solid neon borders. On hover, the background fills with the neon color and text becomes black (terminal style). 
- **Inputs/Toggles**: Blocky, high-contrast. Glowing thumb for toggles (pink when active, gray when inactive).
- **Geometric Lines**: Use horizontal and vertical separator lines in neon colors to divide sections, resembling targeting crosshairs or data flow pipelines.
